# MASTER SPECIFICATION: A STAR LANTERN ACROSS TIME

This document preserves the complete master vision, creative guidelines, and technical rules for the project.

---

## 0. Core Philosophy & Rules
- **Role:** Senior Three.js engineer, Technical artist, Cinematic environment designer, Lighting artist, Interaction designer, Multiplayer engineer, Art director.
- **Vision:** "A playable animated short film about two people connected by one star lantern across time." Personal Mid-Autumn Festival gift for girlfriend.
- **Operating Order:** Explore -> Plan -> Implement vertical slice -> Run -> Inspect visually -> Critique as art director -> Fix top 3 weaknesses -> Re-run and verify -> Only then continue.
- **Priorities:**
  1. Cinematic lighting
  2. Lantern visual quality
  3. Camera choreography
  4. Atmospheric sound
  5. Smooth transition from darkness to the village
- **Cultural Identity:** 100% authentically Vietnamese (clay tile roofs, bamboo hedges, rural mud/adobe walls, sedge mats *chiếu cói*, frog drums *trống ếch*, traditional 5-pointed star lanterns). Avoid generic East Asian, Japanese, or cyberpunk tropes.
- **Scale:** 5–10 minutes total playtime, 8–10 continuous cinematic scenes. Never an open-world game.

---

## 1. Timeline & Color Scripts
### Past (Scenes 0–6)
- **Palette:** Deep midnight navy `#0f172a`, sepia/monochrome memory shift, silver moonbeam `#d7e8ff`, warm amber candlelight `#f59e0b`, bamboo brown, rustic clay red.
- **Lighting:** Darkness is precious. The Moon is the rim light; the candle is the heart.
- **Environment:** Rural northern Vietnamese village, sedge mat on floor, moonbeam through wooden window slats, frog drums in the distance.

### Present (Scenes 7–10)
- **Palette:** Midnight navy, warm gold, coral red, soft cyan, muted purple.
- **Lighting:** Electric festival illuminations, modern star lantern, overlapping lantern light casting star pattern on the ground.

---

## 2. Scenes Master Breakdown
1. **Scene 0: Time Travel Intro**
   - Sepia/monochrome fade, film grain overlay, reverse particle vortex pulling into the center.
   - Clock ticking accelerating, slowing down, dissolving into nocturnal crickets.
   - Serif typography: *"Làng quê Bắc Bộ — Một mùa trăng cuối thế kỷ 20..."*
2. **Scene 1: Dark Room & Lantern Crafting**
   - Darkness (`#0f172a`), single moonbeam through slatted window onto a traditional sedge mat (*chiếu cói*).
   - Materials on the mat: 10 bamboo rods, thin wire, red-orange cellophane sheets with golden borders, bowl of cold rice paste glue.
   - Assembly & ignition: Bloom effect, warm `#f59e0b` glow, casting multi-colored 5-pointed star shadows onto clay walls.
3. **Scene 2: Door Reveal**
   - Camera tracks behind player holding the lantern. Wooden double-doors swing open into moonlit village courtyard with frog drums.
4. **Scene 3: Walk Through Past (2.5D Multiplane Parallax)**
   - Candle wind/flame flickering mechanic, bamboo fences, banyan silhouettes, village folk.
5. **Scene 4-5: Village Festival & Lion Dance**
   - Gesture tracing, lion head dancing.
6. **Scene 6: Memory Rise**
   - Camera pulls up above village into the Moon.
7. **Scene 7-8: Modern Plaza & Second Player**
   - Modern festival plaza, two players with lanterns.
8. **Scene 9-10: Co-op Separation, Gate & Moon Ending**
   - Overcoming separation, opening festival gate, overlapping star light, heartfelt personal message.
